Guided Scenarios (Test the Edge Cases)

Scenario: Alice (ticket author) reports a bug. Bob (assigned developer) picks up the ticket, finishes the code, and marks it resolved. Alice verifies the fix and officially closes it.

Step 1: Bob begins work Actor: Bob Smith (Assignee) → Action: In Progress
Step 2: Bob resolves the issue Actor: Bob Smith (Assignee) → Action: Resolved
Step 3: Alice verifies and closes Actor: Alice Chen (Author) → Action: Closed

Scenario: Charlie is an unassigned peer member in the workspace. In an FSM model, Charlie should NOT be able to prematurely resolve Bob's work or close Alice's ticket without being author, admin, or assignee.

Step 1: Bob starts working Actor: Bob Smith (Assignee) → Status becomes In Progress
Step 2: Bystander Charlie tries to mark Resolved Actor: Charlie Davis (Non-assignee member) → Action: Resolved (Should be BLOCKED)
Step 3: Bob marks Resolved, Charlie tries to Close Actor: Charlie tries to close Alice's ticket → Action: Closed (Should be BLOCKED)

Scenario: A ticket has been closed. Should ordinary members be able to reopen it at will, or should reopening be gated to Workspace Admins and Owners to prevent zombie ticket churn?

Step 1: Setup - Fast forward ticket to Closed Transitions ticket through in_progress → resolved → closed
Step 2: Member Alice tries to reopen Actor: Alice Chen (Member/Author) → Action: Reopen (Should be BLOCKED)
Step 3: Admin Dana reopens Actor: Dana Vance (Admin) → Action: Reopen (Should be ALLOWED)

Scenario: In Option 2 (FSM), shortcuts like jumping directly from open → closed or open → resolved without doing the work are disallowed.

Step 1: Reset ticket to Open Ticket status: open
Step 2: Try jumping directly from Open → Closed Actor: Alice Chen (Author) → Action: Closed (Should be BLOCKED: cannot skip in_progress/resolved)
Step 3: Try jumping directly from Open → Resolved Actor: Bob Smith (Assignee) → Action: Resolved (Should be BLOCKED: must start work first)

Comparison Analysis: Toggle between Option 1 (Freeform) and Option 2 (Role-Gated FSM) to compare developer ergonomics vs governance safety.

Trade-off Assessment:

  • Option 1 (Freeform): Maximum flexibility, zero transition rejections. Drawback: Any member can accidentally close unresolved tickets or bypass team workflow visibility.
  • Option 2 (FSM with Role Gates): Enforces clear accountability. Developers must acknowledge tickets (in_progress), assignees or admins sign off on resolution (resolved), ticket authors accept the delivery (closed), and only leadership can reopen (open).
